# Manually added documents

Put documents here that cannot be downloaded by script, then rebuild the index
(`python scripts/build_index.py`):

- European Commission EUDR Guidance Document (3rd edition, May 2026), PDF
- European Commission EUDR FAQ (5th version, May 2026), PDF

Get them from the Commission's EUDR page (environment.ec.europa.eu, "Regulation on
deforestation-free products"). PDFs are split per page and cited as `<file name> p.N`.
Markdown or text files are split on `## ` headings.
